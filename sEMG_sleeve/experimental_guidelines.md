# Sleeve EMG Experiment Guidelines

## Goal
Collect consistent 10-minute recording sessions for sleeve EMG hand-motion regression.

## General Instructions
- Seat the subject comfortably with the forearm supported.
- Keep the sleeve placement consistent within a session.
- Record synchronized EMG and hand-angle data for the full session.
- Ask the subject to stay relaxed between movement bouts and avoid unnecessary arm motion.
- Encourage full but comfortable range of motion.
- For all tasks, prioritize clean, repeatable movements over maximal effort.

## Session Structure
Total time per session: **10 minutes**

### 1. Straight finger bends (MCP) — slow
**Duration:** 2 minutes

- Focus on bending primarily at the MCP joints (the knuckles).
- Keep the fingers relatively straight while flexing and extending.
- Use slow, controlled repetitions.
- Try to move all fingers clearly through a comfortable range.

### 2. Straight finger bends (MCP) — fast
**Duration:** 2 minutes

- Same MCP-focused motion as above.
- Perform the repetitions faster while keeping the motion clean.
- Avoid excessive wrist or forearm movement.

### 3. PIP finger bends — slow
**Duration:** 1 minute

- Focus on bending the PIP joints (the middle joint of each finger).
- Perform slow, isolated flexion/extension as much as possible.
- Keep motion deliberate and controlled.

### 4. PIP finger bends — fast
**Duration:** 1 minute

- Same PIP-focused motion as above.
- Perform faster repetitions.
- Still aim for distinct joint motion rather than random movement.

### 5. Robust finger movements — slow
**Duration:** 2 minutes

- The subject can perform varied hand and finger motions naturally.
- Use slow movements.
- Encourage a wide variety of realistic hand configurations.
- Include combinations of finger flexion/extension and mixed poses.

### 6. Robust finger movements — fast
**Duration:** 2 minutes

- Perform varied, natural hand gestures at a faster pace.
- Emphasize common real-world gestures, such as:
  - counting gestures
  - thumbs up
  - fist
  - open hand
  - partial grasps
  - mixed finger poses
- Keep the gestures recognizable and varied.
